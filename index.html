Typography

Two faces, seventeen roles. Calluna, a serif, carries titles, ledes and stat values; Source Sans 3 carries body copy, figures and every label.

Sizes are points at the reader's default text size and scale up with the iOS text-size setting. Line height is a multiple of the size. Letter-spacing is in em.

Components

Not for restyling

These have no visual design of their own.

CaptureProtected: hides live content from screenshots and recordings.
FavouritesStore: remembers which modules are starred.
ReleaseVisibility / ReleaseGate: decides whether a surface renders, greys out, or is dropped.
TapReporting / AnalyticsSlug: names controls so taps can be counted.
EngageTapStyle / EngageCardTapStyle: the press feel and haptic on taps.
Loadable: the four load states as a type.
PushLink: a row that pushes a destination onto the stack.
WidgetDescriptor: the service-free description of a module tile.
TypedLayout / MeasuringCell / ScaledChartHeight: measurement helpers.
ScrollsToTop / ScrollsToSection: scroll behaviour.
PassthroughWindow / ToastWindowOverlay: how toasts render above everything.
ScreenshotMode: a mode for capturing marketing screens.
